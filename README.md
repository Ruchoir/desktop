# Ruchoir desktop

The desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux: a light Tauri shell with a bar of products on the
left, one web view per product taking the whole space, and the option to open a product in its own
window.

> In construction: part of the restructuring of Ruchoir into separate products. The app moved here
> from `Ruchoir/ruchoir` with its history; its shell is not rebuilt yet. Layout: [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md).
